## General Tax Credit (Algemene heffingskorting)

### Under AOW age
- Maximum: €3,068
- Phase-out starts at: €28,406 gross
- Phase-out rate: 6.337%
- Formula: €3,068 − 6.337% × (verzamelinkomen − €28,406)
- Reaches €0 at: €76,818 gross

### AOW age or older
- Maximum: €1,536
- Phase-out starts at: €28,406 gross
- Phase-out rate: 3.170%
- Formula: €1,536 − 3.170% × (verzamelinkomen − €28,406)
- Reaches €0 at: €76,818 gross

## Labour Tax Credit (Arbeidskorting)

### Under AOW age
| Income Range           | Formula                                   | Resulting Credit |
|------------------------|-------------------------------------------|------------------|
| €0 – €12,169          | 8.053% × arbeidsinkomen                   | €0 – €980        |
| €12,170 – €26,288     | €980 + 30.030% × (income − €12,169)      | €980 – €5,220    |
| €26,289 – €43,071     | €5,220 + 2.258% × (income − €26,288)     | €5,220 – €5,599  |
| €43,072 – €129,078    | €5,599 − 6.510% × (income − €43,071)     | €5,599 – €0      |
| €129,079+              | €0                                        | €0               |

- Maximum: €5,599 (at income €43,071)
- Build-up rate 1: 8.053% (€0 to €12,169)
- Build-up rate 2: 30.030% (€12,169 to €26,288)
- Build-up rate 3: 2.258% (€26,288 to €43,071)
- Phase-out rate: 6.510% (€43,071 to €129,078)
- Reaches €0 at: €129,079

## Healthcare Contribution (Zvw)
- Employer rate (werkgeversheffing Zvw): 6.51%
- Employee/self-employed rate (bijdrage Zvw): 5.26%
- Ceiling (maximumbijdrageloon): €75,864

**Important**: For standard employees, the employer pays the werkgeversheffing (6.51%). This is NOT deducted from the employee's salary. The 5.26% rate applies to self-employed/DGA. For the gross-to-net employee calculator, the Zvw is an employer cost, not an employee deduction.

Source: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/zorgverzekeringswet/veranderingen-bijdrage-zvw/percentages-zvw

## Edge Cases
- **AOW age**: 67 in 2025. Different premium rates apply (no AOW premium → lower bracket 1 rate of 17.92%)
- **30% ruling (expatregeling)**: Out of scope for v1
- **Self-employed / ZZP**: Out of scope — different Zvw rate (5.26%), no employee insurance premiums
- **Transitional AOW**: People reaching AOW age during 2025 get prorated rates — simplified to binary toggle for v1
